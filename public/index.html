⚡ Supported Formats & Capabilities

NEXADOWN 3D is powered by a high-throughput standalone engine capable of analyzing and extracting video and audio streams:

🎬 Video Streams

  • 4K Ultra HD (2160p): High-bitrate H.264 / AV1 with AAC audio.
  • 2K Quad HD (1440p): Pristine quality for high-resolution monitors.
  • Full HD (1080p @ 60fps): The worldwide streaming standard.
  • HD (720p): Lightweight, optimized for mobile devices.
  • SD (480p & 360p): Low-bandwidth fast-saving formats.

🎵 Audio Streams

  • MP3 (320 kbps): CBR maximum bitrate with embedded lyrics & cover art.
  • WAV (16-bit PCM): Uncompressed studio waveform for DAWs.
  • M4A / AAC: Native container for iOS, macOS, and Android.
  • FLAC: Lossless compressed audio with 100% fidelity.

🌐 Supported Platforms

  • YouTube · Spotify · Instagram · TikTok · Twitter/X · Facebook
  • SoundCloud · Reddit · Twitch · Dailymotion · Vimeo · Bandcamp
  • Direct .mp4 / .webm / .m3u8 / .mkv links
